## What was extracted

Article titles, author names and the publisher's displayed Created and Last updated dates were read from headings and Article Information panels. These dates are not assumed to be first publication dates or dates of underlying studies. Each record links to its original page and documents inspected sections.

Topic tags and brief discovery descriptions were written by AI from inspected headings and introductory material. They are not publisher metadata or copied publisher summaries. The publisher separately discloses AI-assisted editing and AI-generated summaries/highlights; that disclosure is recorded without asserting that the authored article body is AI-generated.

Bibliographies, study identifiers and original papers were not extracted or appraised. The empty identifier lists mean not collected, not absence of references. No primary-study result, effect size or causal conclusion is represented by this pilot.

## Extraction and reuse scope

Seven selected public pages were inspected through web retrieval. No site-wide crawl or complete index export was performed. A request to the publisher's robots file returned HTTP 403; its directives remain unverified, and no access bypass was attempted. That response neither establishes a licence nor clears automated harvesting.

The site states all rights reserved. Its [Style Guide](https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/psi-encyclopedia-style-guide/) transfers article rights to SPR and permits authors to republish their own contributions; no blanket third-party reuse licence was verified. The packet retains factual metadata, links and original descriptions, with no article text, publisher summaries/highlights, images or PDFs. Collection-wide expansion still requires a confirmed extraction/reuse route, including applicable database-right and terms checks. Attribution alone is not reuse permission.

## Evidence and governance

Seven encyclopedia records add zero independent studies or replications, zero accepted edges and no model-support mappings. A topic overview and the study it describes are one evidence lineage; overlaps across entries require study and dataset deduplication. Critical discussions here remain within the same publisher and are not assumed to be independent external appraisals. All records require review; all promotion gates stay closed and Big Question statuses are unchanged. No merge or website publication is part of this pilot.
