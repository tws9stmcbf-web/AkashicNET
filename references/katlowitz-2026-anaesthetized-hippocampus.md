## ❓ Research Question

What kinds of neural processing and plasticity can occur in the human hippocampus during general anaesthesia?

## 📊 Relevance

The research is relevant to questions about the relationship between neural processing and conscious awareness.

It provides an opportunity to examine whether complex information processing can persist when ordinary conscious responsiveness is substantially disrupted.

## ⚖️ Evidence Status

**Emerging evidence**

The findings should not be interpreted as evidence that neural processing during anaesthesia necessarily constitutes conscious experience.

## 🧐 Critical Reading

Important distinctions:

**Neural activity ≠ automatically conscious experience**

**Information processing ≠ automatically subjective awareness**

The study should therefore be interpreted within its actual experimental design and limitations.
