# AkashicNET rights-candidate triage policy v0.37

## Milestone
Advance real corpus rights research as far as current evidence safely permits without reading unauthorised Drive document bodies or converting work-level public-domain evidence into manifestation-level permission.

## Result of this pass
Three historically plausible candidates were reviewed at work level. Each remains `UNKNOWN_UNVERIFIED` and public-manifest ineligible.

No real corpus item reached R2, R3 or R4.

## Evidence sources used
External bibliographic and rights signals may establish that a named historical work/edition is a strong rights-review candidate. They do not prove that a Drive object is the same edition, scan or manifestation.

For `Alchemy Ancient and Modern`, external records identify H. Stanley Redgrove's second revised edition as London, William Rider & Son, 1922. Project Gutenberg states that the print edition underlying its ebook is not protected by U.S. copyright law. This supports work/edition-level candidacy only.

For `Egyptian Ideas of the Future Life`, external text records identify E. A. Wallis Budge and a London preface dated 21 August 1899. This supports historical/public-domain investigation, not exact Drive manifestation identity.

For `The Book of Am-Tuat`, external records identify E. A. Wallis Budge, London, Kegan Paul, Trench, Trübner & Co., 1905, and public web editions describe that historical text as public domain. This does not transfer the rights status of those web manifestations onto the Drive object.

## Why R2 is blocked
Current allowed repository metadata does not establish the exact edition/scan/derivative content of the corresponding Drive PDFs. Filename, title, collection path, size equality, old publication date, or a public-domain copy elsewhere are insufficient.

Resolving this blocker could require one of:
- richer pre-existing manifestation metadata that identifies edition/publisher/year without document-body access;
- a separately authorised inspection of title/copyright pages for a narrowly specified file;
- a cryptographic match to an independently rights-cleared manifestation where both sides can be lawfully hashed.

This pass does not grant any new raw Drive access.

## Jurisdiction caution
Copyright status is jurisdiction-specific. A U.S. public-domain signal is not automatically global redistribution permission. R4 still requires explicit jurisdiction/scope evidence and independent corroboration under the v0.30 policy.
