## Summary

This project tracks the r/NeuronsToNirvana Community Archive as a provenance-first, evidence-aware catalogue.

The validated pilot remains the baseline for Stage 1. The Stage 1 goal is to reach 250 curated, reviewable records while preserving the refined schema and taxonomy established in the pilot.

## Scope

- Preserve original Reddit URLs.
- Preserve attribution where publicly available.
- Do not reproduce copyrighted post text or images unnecessarily.
- Separate source type, category, lived experience, and evidence status.
- Link relevant external sources when they help contextualise the archive item.
- Do not turn community material into scientific evidence.
- Flag uncertain provenance rather than guessing.
- Deduplicate where appropriate.
- Keep a running accurate count for future expansion.

## Expansion rules for Stage 1

- Use the refined schema already validated in the pilot.
- Keep classification fields distinct: source_type, category, evidence_status.
- Maintain lived_experience as a separate evidence status when appropriate.
- Use toolkit_framework only when genuinely applicable.
- Record provenance and reuse risk explicitly.
- Prefer links to original material over reposting content.
- Do not broaden scope beyond the archive's provenance and evidence safeguards.
