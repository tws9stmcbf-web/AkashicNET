# Rights Audit Research Batch 0002

Date: 2026-08-29

## Result

No imported Drive object is promoted to `PUBLIC_VERIFIED` in this batch.

Authoritative research confirms that Project Gutenberg's US clearance is useful evidence only when the exact manifestation is tied to a qualifying Gutenberg record or equivalent authoritative rights evidence. Project Gutenberg states that qualifying US works published in 1930 or earlier are unrestricted under its Rule 1 as of 2026, but also warns that editions and translations can have independent copyright. Therefore author death dates or ancient underlying works are insufficient by themselves.

## Evidence policy adopted

- A historical work is a **candidate**, not cleared, until the actual manifestation/edition is identified.
- A Project Gutenberg record may support a `PUBLIC_VERIFIED` determination for the specific Gutenberg electronic work in the US when its copyright status and licence terms are documented; it does not automatically clear an unrelated Drive scan.
- Non-US distribution requires jurisdiction-specific review; this project must not silently convert US public-domain status into global clearance.
- Public-domain status and scientific/evidential status remain independent dimensions.

## Current triage

Strongest candidates remain historical works by authors such as Dostoevsky and H. G. Wells, older reference works, and historical religious texts. However, the current Drive metadata does not yet identify enough edition/translation detail to attach authoritative rights evidence to individual Drive objects.

Accordingly all such candidates remain `UNKNOWN_UNVERIFIED`.
