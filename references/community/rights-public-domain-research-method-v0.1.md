# AKASHICNET-006 — Rights Audit Research Method v0.1

Date: 2026-08-29

## First-pass evidence standard

For public-domain candidates, record work title, author, author death year where relevant, first publication year, edition/translation year, jurisdiction, authoritative source, evidence URL, access date, and reviewer decision.

A work may be marked `PUBLIC_VERIFIED` only when the evidence is specific enough to support the intended jurisdiction/use. A general statement that an author is old is insufficient where a later translation, edition, annotation, introduction, illustration or typesetting may have independent rights.

## AkashicNET rule

These external rules are evidence for the audit methodology, not automatic clearance for any individual Drive object. The actual object/edition must be identified before promotion to `PUBLIC_VERIFIED`.

## Current score impact

No rights points are awarded by this methodology document alone. The final 10-point gate remains pending object-level evidence and reconciliation.
