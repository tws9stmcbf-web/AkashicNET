## Source checks

The machine-readable [source review](consciousness-library-source-review-20261016.json) records field origins, upstream providers, rights exclusions, corrections, date precision and limits. The [methods](https://consciousnesslibrary.org/methods), [technical pipeline](https://consciousnesslibrary.org/docs/article-pipeline.html) and [terms](https://consciousnesslibrary.org/terms) were inspected. Provider names at platform level do not establish an individual record's importing provider; unknown per-record origins remain null.

Observed year controls accept 1900–2027. A bounded historical query returned a mescaline record whose DOI metadata dates it to June 1913; the library displays a first day that Crossref does not independently specify. A 2026 record is already in the public guide. These examples establish neither the full date range nor completeness. Undated/pre-1900 holdings and complete pagination remain unchecked. Catalogue totals are deliberately not frozen.

The terms do not establish an open grant to republish platform summaries or bulk-export its database. This update uses source links, independently checked bibliographic facts and original AkashicNET review notes. No abstracts, AI summaries, syntheses, figures or full texts are copied. [Crossref's metadata policy](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/) distinguishes bibliographic facts from abstracts and their separate rights.

## Correction and date checks that matter

The compassion paper's [primary erratum](https://doi.org/10.1093/scan/nsab068), also checked as [repository XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC8483276/fullTextXML), fixes the title's singular/plural wording. Its Crossref main record nevertheless returned no update relation. Empty provider warnings are not proof of no correction. The preprint relationship is preserved without counting it as replication.

The candidate annotation retains the stronger comparison against familiarity and the uncorrected placebo comparison from the original publisher abstract. It does not infer a cosmic field. Further full-paper, replication and human appraisal remain pending.

Online, issue and corrected/typeset dates are retained separately. Europe PMC's issue-based first-publication field for the dream paper differs from Crossref's online date; neither is silently substituted. The AWARE Crossref request received HTTP 429; its successful verification used PubMed and the existing canonical record, not a claimed Crossref result.

For source errors, the library supplies its contact page and `admin@consciousnesslibrary.org`. No message was sent. AkashicNET corrections should retain the work identity, notice relationship and version history and require human review before evidence changes.

## Review and validation boundary

JSON parsing, DOI identity checks, original-registry preservation and existing BQ architecture/status validators are the relevant checks for this metadata-only change. Public-site publication requires its own successful build and deployment receipt. No scientific conclusion is certified by passing software checks.

Remaining gaps: authoritative whole-catalogue coverage; bulk/API reuse permission; full-text and independent-replication appraisal of the new candidate; human approval before evidence promotion.
