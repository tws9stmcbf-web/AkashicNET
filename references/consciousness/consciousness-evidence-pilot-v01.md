# Consciousness Evidence Trail: Five-Reading Pilot

A bounded manual extension to PRISM intake, AkashicOMNI review and AkashicNET editorial practice. The [five worked records](consciousness-evidence-pilot-v01.json) cover readings selected from 10 October to 7 November 2026. These are review annotations, not accepted knowledge-graph edges or a software release.

1. **PRISM:** Record the source, exact task or stimulus where available, who was observed, what was measured and when. Separate observations from reports, hypothetical examples and model inputs. Use “not reported” when a field cannot be established.
2. **AkashicOMNI:** Identify whether a claim concerns detection, access for use or report, subjective experience or reflection on experience. Use multiple tags if needed; these are dimensions of a claim, not a maturity ranking. Record the relevant level of description, assumptions, alternative explanations and what would discriminate between them.
3. **AkashicNET:** State the best supported conclusion and its limits in plain language. Treat evidence confidence and reasons to act under uncertainty as separate judgements. Preserve the original source and link the review.
4. **BQ001:** Distinguish the time of a later report, the inferred time of the experience and the separate claim that awareness continued. This pilot supplies no new evidence of continuity and does not alter BQ001’s unresolved status.

## Acceptance For A Wider Rollout

A second reviewer should independently classify each record. Compare disagreements about what was measured and what is being inferred. Revise ambiguous fields before reusing the method across the corpus. Existing ratings and promotion controls stay as they are.
