## Insights, Intelligence, Knowledge and Wisdom

- **Insights:** make assumptions visible before drawing connections.
- **Intelligence:** compare alternative explanations and conditions under which they predict different outcomes.
- **Knowledge:** preserve source, attribution, date, scope and evidence class.
- **Wisdom:** listen, accept correction and connect inquiry with compassionate conduct. Compassion is an ethical commitment, not an experimental confirmation of a theory.

Use AkashicOMNI's existing DISCERN and DEEPEN passes as a manual framework. The v0.5.0 public method and the separate engineering pilot have different statuses; this addition changes neither. See [public status](../../docs/AKASHICOMNI_V050_PUBLIC_STATUS.md).

## Proposed First Pilot

Select one claim with a willing team. Record:

1. Original source and exact scope.
2. Operational definitions and comparison model.
3. Predicted outcome and uncertainty.
4. Controls, blinding and plausible confounds.
5. Data access, provenance and analysis plan.
6. A result that would require revision.
7. Independent replication status.
8. Permission before attributing unpublished comments.

All are unfilled requirements until a study is selected. No access, resources, contact or partner are assumed.
