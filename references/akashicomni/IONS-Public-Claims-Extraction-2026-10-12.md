## Interpretation and review

Reported statistical departures from a null do not identify an information-transfer mechanism. Chance, model misspecification, selection, dependence, analytical flexibility and ordinary information leakage are review alternatives, not demonstrated explanations or accusations. Likewise a nonsignificant condition is not proof of absence. No pooled effect or inter-condition significance test is calculated. Proposed mechanisms require discriminating, independently replicated tests with appropriate controls.

No systematic counter-evidence review was completed. A publisher search excerpt's broad genetic-relatedness highlight differs in emphasis from the telephone abstract's regression findings. That endpoint is excluded pending full-text reconciliation; it does not change the two extracted accuracy endpoints. Independent methodological review, protocol/preregistration checks and human source attestations remain pending.
