# 🔬 Research

This directory collects and organises research relevant to the Noetic Sciences Toolkit.

## Purpose

The aim is to create a transparent bridge between interdisciplinary research and the practical frameworks developed in this project.

## Areas of Research

- 🧠 Consciousness
- 🧬 Neuroscience
- 🧘 Contemplative science
- 🧪 Psychedelic research
- 🧠 Psychology
- 🏃 Human performance and wellbeing
- 🌍 Human–environment relationships
- 🤖 Artificial intelligence and consciousness
- 🧩 Systems and complexity science

## Evidence Framework

Research should be classified where appropriate as:

- **Established evidence**
- **Emerging evidence**
- **Working hypothesis**
- **Speculative**
- **Historical / cultural context**

## Source Standards

Whenever possible:

1. Prefer primary research.
2. Identify peer-reviewed sources.
3. Link to the original publication.
4. Record publication details.
5. Distinguish correlation from causation.
6. Record important limitations.
7. Note conflicting evidence.
8. Avoid overstating conclusions.
