# BQ discovery catalogue through Batch 16

91 unique source records, including 35 Psi Encyclopedia entries. These are discovery records, not 91 validated independent studies.

The catalogue links research on consciousness, spontaneous thought, compassion, distant intention and brain synchrony to exploratory Big Question queries. Descriptions and relevance mappings are original AI-assisted notes. Publisher metadata and access checks have the inspection limits recorded per entry.

## Files

- `catalogue.json`: consolidated current metadata through Batch 16.
- `batch-13.json` through `batch-16.json`: additive batch snapshots.
- `validate.py`: repeatable integrity and governance checks.
- `validation.json`: result of the checked export.

The prior source-version wrappers are omitted from the public export; record hashes and input-file hash identify the saved originating snapshot. No emails, correspondence, private research trace, source bodies, comments, media or raw participant data are included.

## Evidence and rights

All entries require review. Zero independent studies promoted, zero accepted edges, empty model support, and no change to Big Question maturity or website status. Multiple publications about one cohort must remain one evidence lineage; reviews and historical reanalyses are not new experimental confirmations.

Access is article-specific. Free reading does not establish a reuse licence. Article licences do not automatically cover datasets or third-party material. The CC BY-NC-ND notice for the distant-healing overview is preserved as a distinct restriction. No blanket source permissions are asserted. Author lists and publication dates retain explicit incompleteness or version semantics where needed.

The conventional social-interaction studies are comparators with sensory communication; they are not evidence of communication without sensory channels. Null findings and methodological criticism are retained alongside positive reports.

Run from this directory:

```sh
python3 validate.py
```

This research snapshot is not connected to a public website route or ingestion pipeline. Repository storage does not itself validate or publish a scientific finding.
