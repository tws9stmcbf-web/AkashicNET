# 🔺 Interconnected Pyramids — Reflection Tool

## Purpose

A simple reflective tool for exploring which areas of life may currently need attention.

It uses the **Many Pyramids, One Blueprint** concept as a flexible map rather than a fixed hierarchy.

## 🧩 Six Domains

Rate each domain from **1–5** based on your current experience.

| Domain | Question | Rating |
|---|---|---|
| 🧱 Foundations | Do I have sufficient safety, stability and basic resources? | /5 |
| ❤️ Relationships | Do I feel connected, supported and able to communicate? | /5 |
| 🧠 Mind | Am I learning, thinking clearly and engaging creatively? | /5 |
| 🏃 Body | Am I supporting my physical wellbeing, movement and recovery? | /5 |
| 🧘 Consciousness | Am I cultivating awareness, attention and self-understanding? | /5 |
| ✨ Purpose | Do my activities feel meaningful and aligned with my values? | /5 |

## 🔍 Reflect

After rating the domains, ask:

1. Which area is strongest?
2. Which area needs the most attention?
3. Is one area affecting another?
4. Am I focusing on a “higher” goal while neglecting a foundation?
5. What is one small change I could test?
6. What would I measure to see whether that change helps?

## 🔄 Experiment

Choose **one** area.

Define:

**Current observation:**  
What is happening now?

**Small intervention:**  
What could I change?

**Time period:**  
How long will I observe the change?

**Measure:**  
What will I track?

**Result:**  
What happened?

**Learning:**  
What would I change next time?

## ⚖️ Important Note

This is a **reflection and self-observation tool**, not a diagnostic instrument or scientifically validated psychological assessment.

The ratings are subjective observations and should not be treated as objective measurements of wellbeing or human development.

## 🌱 Guiding Principle

> **You don't always need to climb higher. Sometimes you need to strengthen the foundation.**

**Observe → Reflect → Experiment → Learn → Adapt**
