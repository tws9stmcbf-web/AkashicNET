# 🤝 Contributing to the Noetic Sciences Toolkit

Thank you for helping build the Toolkit.

Contributions are welcome across research, documentation, frameworks, visualisations, experiments and practical tools.

## 🔬 What Can You Contribute?

- Research and references
- Critical reviews
- New frameworks or models
- Citizen-science experiments
- Lifestyle and reflection tools
- Visualisations
- Documentation
- Corrections and improvements

## ⚖️ Evidence Matters

Please distinguish clearly between:

- **Evidence**
- **Observation**
- **Interpretation**
- **Hypothesis**
- **Speculation**

Unconventional ideas are welcome, but they should not be presented as established scientific facts without appropriate evidence.

## 🧠 Critique Is Welcome

The Toolkit is **pro-inquiry, not pro-belief**.

Constructive criticism, alternative explanations and attempts to reproduce findings are valuable contributions.

## 🌱 Contribution Principle

> **Explore freely. Document carefully. Question openly. Revise when the evidence changes.**

## 🚀 Simple Workflow

**Explore → Question → Research → Create → Document → Share → Review → Improve**

Thank you for helping develop an open and evolving knowledge commons.
