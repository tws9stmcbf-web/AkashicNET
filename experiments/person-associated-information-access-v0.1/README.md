## 2. Separate the claims before testing them

| Claim | Observable prediction | Evidential limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary cognitive effect | Instructions or learned dossiers change factual recall, inference, confidence or felt connection. | A psychological effect, not inaccessible information. |
| Hidden-information correspondence | A locked choice matches an independently selected concealed target more often than its randomized null permits. | Challenges the specified independence model if integrity is sound; does not identify the route. |
| Mettā-specific effect | Trained-person hidden accuracy exceeds both chance and trained-person accuracy under ordinary reflection. | An instruction-package effect; cannot isolate love, attention, expectation or an alleged energy. |
| Representation moderation | The mettā-minus-reflection difference is larger for enriched than minimally cued person representations. | Dossier learning is a behavioral proxy, not proof of a particular neural pathway. Pilot interaction is exploratory. |
| Source identity | The actual named historical individual supplied information. | Not identified by this pilot, even if all performance predictions succeed. |
| Physical 7D store | A defined physical model predicts a measurable effect different from specified rivals. | No such quantitative coupling is supplied here; physical test remains unspecified. |

**Bridge assumption accepted for pilot design on 18 October 2026:** A newly randomized symbol stored in a record associated with a person's dossier is predicted to be accessible through the proposed process, although it was not part of that person's lifetime knowledge. The original hypothesis does not logically entail this assumption. A random symbol is a deliberately stringent surrogate, not a faithful model of an Einstein insight by default.

The proponent's agreement to use the bridge is now recorded. Necessary conditions, instruction fidelity and a minimum meaningful effect still require explicit specification and review before registration. Agreement makes the assumption part of the proposed design; it does not show that a random symbol faithfully represents historical knowledge. If the proponent later limits the hypothesis to information possessed during the person's lifetime, redesign the information module before data collection. Do not reinterpret a null symbol result as disproving all forms of the original hypothesis.

An alternative historical-information module would require independently authenticated, prospectively chosen archival facts, dated access records and appropriate rights. Unpublished does not mean inaccessible: relatives, archivists, investigators or training corpora may already contain the answer. Such a module has no guaranteed 25% null and needs a separate protocol. No archived secret, bereaved participant or historical contact claim is tested here.

## 3. Define conventional availability

| Information class | Examples and control | What accuracy means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supplied or previously encountered | Dossiers, books, lectures, videos, conversation. Log provided materials; baseline familiarity and source-recognition measures. | Recall, including forgotten-source memory, remains plausible. |
| Inferable | Consequences of supplied facts, recognizable patterns, educated guessing. Use a separate prespecified inference answer key. | Reasoning can generate correct knowledge never memorized verbatim. |
| Shared culture and semantics | Famous quotations, stereotypes, archetypes, Einstein imagery. Include matched cues and complete false-statement accounting. | Similarity and fluency do not establish shared external access. |
| Generative-AI familiarity | Previous chatbot use, model-generated prose and training-data exposure. No AI in the session; record minimal prior exposure without collecting private chat histories. | A training cutoff, failed web search or denial of familiarity cannot establish novelty. |
| Public but withheld fact | Biographical detail omitted from a dossier. Keep as an ordinary-information challenge. | A hit is not an inaccessible-information result. |
| Fresh hidden assignment | Independently randomized current symbol for each unique participant–person–trial, committed before response and never sent to the response system. | Exact matching can test the specified independence null under audited access assumptions. |

“Unavailable” means unavailable through the conventional channels covered by the documented threat model. Absolute absence of every possible channel cannot be proven.

Under the intended null, for each committed trial, P(target = k | dossier, culture, previous responses, public cues) = 1/4. All four symbols may be familiar; the **new secret assignment**, rather than the symbols themselves, carries two bits of uncertainty. Prior knowledge cannot predict that assignment if the independence and concealment assumptions hold. This is a conditional mathematical statement, not an empirical guarantee that the implementation is secure.

## 4. Proposed pilot and prospective selection

**Purpose:** feasibility and estimation, with prespecified signal tests. This is not a definitive confirmatory study or a powered test of the full interaction.

**Sample:** 48 consenting adults, one enrollment each, randomly assigned 24 to mettā contemplation (M) and 24 to ordinary analytical reflection (R). A custodian generates a uniformly random 24/24 allocation before enrollment, concealed from enrollment staff. Stop at 48 randomized participants or eight weeks after first enrollment, whichever comes first. No replacement of withdrawals and no outcome-based extension. Failure to reach 48 is an incomplete feasibility pilot with descriptive results only under this version.

**Person materials:** eight fixed, rights-reviewed historical public-person dossiers, selected and hashed by a stimulus team before recruitment. Avoid relatives, recently deceased personal contacts, restricted sacred knowledge and distressing material. Einstein is reserved for a separately labeled public-knowledge calibration example, excluded from hidden-target inference. The [draft stimulus appendix](materials/README.md) now contains eight proposed dossiers, fact/inference keys and source locators. Independent source/rights review, workload and accessibility checks and final stimulus approval remain launch blockers. Its purposive Nobel-laureate set is not culturally or psychometrically matched.

Each participant receives enriched learning for a uniformly selected four of the eight people, independently of arm and hidden targets. These are the trained condition (T); the remaining four receive name plus one neutral cue (U). U means minimally cued, not absence of neural representation. The fixed eight-person set limits generalization; figures are not eight independent study replications.

**Day 0:** complete consent and the open-target comprehension screening described below before randomization. Then measure baseline person familiarity (0–4), a brief fixed knowledge inventory using items distinct from the final test, and optional general AI/reading exposure. Give no correct-answer feedback on the inventory. Each T learning slot is three minutes total: 150 seconds of dossier study followed by two 15-second retrieval items, without feedback or coaching. Each U slot is 15 seconds of name/cue followed by 165 seconds of unrelated filler. Do not assign U statements about the person to fill the time. These timing choices are proposals for review; failed learning remains in intention-to-treat analysis.

**Day 1, 24 ± 4 hours later:** measure delayed recognition without corrective feedback, administer the assigned five-minute instruction, and conduct 16 hidden trials. Each person appears twice with a fresh target per trial: eight T and eight U trials. Use a recorded independent random permutation of the multiset of person IDs. Targets are never balanced within sessions: exact balancing could make later answers predictable. The learning–test delay is a pragmatic proxy for retained knowledge, not proof of long-term neuronal changes. Out-of-window sessions remain in the ledger and planned-denominator analysis, with a prespecified timing-deviation flag.

**Hidden targets:** the fixed candidate set is circle, triangle, square and plus sign, canonical codes 0–3. For every planned trial the isolated custodian system samples one code uniformly and independently using reviewed cryptographic randomness. Select and commit all 16 assignments immediately before the session and before any corresponding response. Store each with its person ID, opaque trial ID, protocol/stimulus hashes and a fresh secret 256-bit salt. No target redraw for an “uninspired” participant, technical inconvenience, duplicate choice or unremarkable answer.

No designated human sender attends to the target. Classification is RV for the exact inaccessible-information proposition. Staff access is still a possible conventional route; assigning an RV label does not rule out other anomalous models.

## 5. Instructions, trial order and controls

Candidate scripts for cultural, accessibility and methods review follow. [session-scripts.md](materials/session-scripts.md) specifies the full proposed timed tracks, questionnaires, trial prompts and neutral debrief. Text is drafted; actual audio and the participant application are not implemented or approved.

**Shared opening:** “This task compares two ways of attending to information about people. Neither method is known to reveal hidden targets. Please follow your assigned instruction, record any impressions without embellishment, and make one choice. You may report no impression or stop.”

**M:** “Bring this person to mind using only the information available to you. Adopt an attitude of goodwill and compassion toward them. Notice thoughts or images without treating them as messages or facts. Record what occurred, then choose one symbol.”

**R:** “Bring this person to mind using only the information available to you. Review what you know, distinguish facts from guesses, and consider possible associations. Record what occurred, then choose one symbol.”

Audio duration, speaker, setting, cue display, response time and reminders must match. Quiet breathing is natural in both arms; no breath manipulation. Sacred-geometry art, music and ritual objects are absent in both conditions. A later geometry manipulation would require a separate factorial design with matched visual complexity and expectation controls.

Participants cannot be blinded to the instruction they hear. Assess expected effectiveness before and after the script; do not adjust away this post-randomization measure in the primary analysis. An observed arm contrast concerns the whole instruction package. Staff use neutral language and do not certify a state as “authentic.”

Before hidden trials, explain identically to both arms: “A computer has randomly stored one of four symbols in the sealed record for each person and trial. Try to identify that symbol. This is a new experimental association; it is not a claim about the person's actual thoughts or possessions.”

Each hidden trial:

1. Show the person cue for 15 seconds; no truth information reaches this screen.
2. Allow up to 60 seconds of free response, explicitly allowing “no impression.” Lock original text before displaying the response alternatives.
3. Display all four symbols in an independently randomized spatial order. Allow 15 seconds for exactly one choice or abstention; timeout is recorded.
4. Record confidence (0–100), source attribution (memory / inference / spontaneous impression / other / unsure) and felt connection (0–10), without leading source-identity suggestions. Preserve the locked answer.
5. Give no correctness feedback. After trials 4, 8 and 12, offer a scheduled two-minute break and a one-minute reminder of the assigned method. Additional breaks are logged, not grounds for selective exclusion.

After hidden trials, administer all eight fixed dossier-recall items, then all eight fixed inference items, one of each per person. Present four alternatives for each plus a do-not-know route; precommit answer keys and distractors independently of participant responses. Distinguish supplied-fact recall from inference about newly supplied fictional premises. Randomize answer positions independently. The proposed distractors and difficulty still require independent review. These tasks characterize ordinary cognition and manipulation quality; their scores are not pooled with hidden-target scores.

At the start of Day 0, two **open-target calibration** trials visibly present the correct symbol, followed by the same response interface. They test comprehension and coding, not psi. Failures prompt one standardized explanation and two fresh calibration examples before arm exposure; persistently unable participants are screened out before randomization. Record all screened exclusions. No hidden calibration trials are used to select “gifted” participants.

Free text has **no hit score in this pilot**. It is retained, with consent, for a blinded descriptive codebook review. Two independent raters classify source attribution, specific factual assertions, contradictions and unverifiable assertions under a frozen manual. Report both ratings and disagreements; neither rater learns target truth or arm. Text similarity, image resemblance, embeddings or LLM judgments cannot rescue a wrong forced choice. A future free-response experiment needs prospective decoys, a frozen scoring rubric and its own multiplicity plan.

## 6. Blinding, custody and leakage prevention

| Role | Access before final locks | Prohibited access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stimulus team | Dossiers, factual keys, candidate symbols | Participant responses and hidden assignments |
| Randomization/target custodian | Secret allocations and target records | Session contact, prompting, scoring or selective exclusions |
| Participant | Assigned script, allowed dossiers/cues and all four answer alternatives | True hidden code, salts, prior feedback |
| Facilitator | Neutral session controls and response-completion status | Target truth; keep arm masked where possible through headphones |
| Raters | De-identified free responses and codebook | Arm, familiarity allocation, true target and outcomes |
| Analyst | Masked arm labels, trial ledger, locked scoring code; no target join | Truth until data cleaning, exclusions and analysis are frozen |
| Independent auditor | Restricted access needed to verify custody | Participant coaching or changing outcomes |

Prepare independent, encrypted custody and response stores. The response application must never receive the true target in HTML, JSON, caches, network messages, filenames, clipboard, accessibility text, logs or analytics. Disable external browsing, AI assistance, notifications and personal-device use during supervised testing; accommodations require equivalent controls. No cameras, reflections, audio cues or target-dependent delays may disclose truth. Custodian personnel do not enter sessions or communicate target-specific details.

Publish a timestamped commitment to each salted canonical target record before the session; hold the salts and encrypted records under independent dual control. A bare hash of one of four codes is insecure because all possibilities can be tried. A hash establishes neither secrecy nor genuine time ordering by itself: require independent timestamp receipts, logged custody, synchronized clock uncertainty and append-only counters. If timing intervals overlap, temporal ordering is unresolved. Freeze the serialization and cryptographic implementation during engineering review; do not invent cryptography.

Lock all responses, statuses and the statistical analysis code before unmasking. Raters finish before target truth is joined. Verify commitments and all join keys; preserve failed verifications. The auditor checks the actual deployment, not just this document. Pin software versions and hashes, replay synthetic failure cases and inspect retries, recovery files, delayed responses, allocation access and audit logs. NIST guidance [N6] is a reference for reviewed randomness, not a certificate for this proposed system.

No feedback or target opening until the whole pilot's response and analysis locks. If an accidental opening occurs, record its extent and time immediately. Stop accrual for a critical or systemic breach; retain every outcome that may lawfully be retained. Do not silently repair or remove a favorable contaminated trial.

## 7. Scoring, estimands and analysis

These are **candidate rules specified in advance**, not rules already independently approved. An independent statistician must sign them and reproduce the code before registration.

### Exact scoring and denominator

For each planned hidden trial, Y = 1 only when a single locked canonical choice equals its verified pre-response target and the trial passes the frozen integrity checks. Otherwise Y = 0 in the conservative planned-opportunity analysis. Blank, timeout, abstention, incomplete session, invalid target receipt and documented trial breach never disappear from the 16-slot ledger.

Keep their original status separately. Zero assignment means “no verified success in this planned opportunity,” not a claim about the participant's latent ability. It can reduce the success rate below 25%. Report a valid-observation analysis and best/worst missing-data bounds alongside it. Any identified leakage blocks a positive scientific interpretation, even if zero-scoring the affected rows leaves a low p-value.

All 48 randomized participants and 768 planned opportunities remain in the flow ledger. Each has eight T and eight U opportunities. No deletion of participants for low recognition, lack of conviction, a poor session, low confidence, wrong answers or post hoc “psi missing.”

Withdrawal rights take precedence over the ledger. Store only lawfully retainable aggregate disposition counts when individual data must be erased. Do not reconstruct withdrawn responses. If the approved consent/data policy cannot support the fixed randomized denominator, declare this primary analysis not evaluable; do not silently switch denominators. Pre-randomization screening failures are reported separately.

### Co-primary signal tests

Let p_MT and p_RT be the means of participants' eight-slot trained-person accuracy in M and R.

- **H1:** p_MT exceeds 0.25. Conditional on clean independent target draws and no target feedback, exact upper-tail binomial calculation over 192 M/T opportunities is the reference null. Participant response habits do not defeat this randomized-target null, because each target is an independent new draw. Missing opportunities coded zero make this calibration conservative; unresolved leakage invalidates the premise.
- **H2:** p_MT − p_RT exceeds zero. Use a participant-level randomization test respecting the actual uniform 24/24 allocation. Hold each participant's entire trained outcome vector together; resample 24 of 48 assignments, not individual trials. Use 100,000 draws, fixed analysis seed 20261018 and p = (1 + count[T* >= Tobs]) / (100001). Report Monte Carlo standard error.

The prespecified **mettā-specific signal** requires both one-sided p-values <= 0.025, positive contrasts, completed denominator and the integrity/feasibility gates below. This intersection–union rule does not license choosing whichever test succeeds. H2 is a Fisher test of the sharp no-instruction-effect null; it is not a general test that every heterogeneous treatment effect is positive.

The 192-trial exact-binomial shortcut is justified by independent randomized hidden labels, **not** by treating repeated people as independent biological samples. An implementation that balances targets, provides feedback, reuses secret assignments, adaptively selects trials or changes the allocation must use a new preregistered null model.

### Secondary and exploratory reporting

Report all four cell estimates MT, MU, RT and RU, and the interaction (MT − MU) − (RT − RU). Estimate ordinary recall and inference contrasts separately. Report manipulation checks, confidence calibration, felt connection, baseline familiarity, prior AI exposure, figure-specific patterns and attrition by arm. The interaction is a key future discriminator of the representation-dependent version but is **exploratory here**. A positive interaction alone does not satisfy either co-primary test.

Provide two-sided 95% percentile intervals from 20,000 participant bootstrap resamples **within arm**, preserving paired T/U and all trials; seed 20261019. These describe participant-sampling uncertainty, may be unstable in a small pilot and are not a proof of mechanism or exact randomization intervals. Report binomial intervals only as explicitly conditional supplementary intervals if their assumptions are appropriate. All secondary p-values, if computed, are labeled exploratory with the complete family listed; no extra confirmatory claim or subgroup rescue.

No best-participant or best-session analysis replaces the registered result. New patterns go into a dated hypothesis ledger for independent data.

### Sample-size limits

The proposed 48-person pilot is a feasibility choice. Under an ideal homogeneous, independent-hit planning model, H1 needs at least **61/192** hits (31.77%) at alpha 0.025: null upper tail approximately 0.020522. H1-only power is approximately 32.10% at a true rate of 0.30 and 84.47% at 0.35. This is **not** power for H1 and H2 together, the interaction, a heterogeneous population, dropout, or a replication.

The reference script reproduces these calculations. Before launch, independent simulation must examine heterogeneity, attrition, learning failure, implementation faults, H2 and the interaction. A later adequately powered confirmatory study must set a justified smallest effect of interest and fixed sample using new data; this pilot is not reused as its confirmation.

## 8. Feasibility, stopping and failure reporting

All thresholds are proposed review criteria, not observed accomplishments:

- At least 90% of randomized participants complete all 16 hidden choices.
- At least 95% of the 768 planned opportunities have verified pre-response commitments and response/status locks.
- Zero unresolved critical concealment, allocation, identity-join or timing breaches.
- A blinded manipulation report documents whether T actually increased retained knowledge relative to U. Failure limits the representation test; it cannot trigger selective exclusions or redefining familiarity after outcomes.
- Two analysts reproduce exact scores and primary conclusions from the frozen, authorized export.

Integrity criteria outrank numerical success. Monitor only safety, enrollment and integrity, never running accuracy. Stop immediately for serious distress or an unresolved systemic breach; do not continue until governance review approves a new version. No efficacy stopping or hidden extensions. Record all screened, eligible, consented, randomized, attempted, completed, withdrawn, unscorable and breached counts. Each person–trial pair appears exactly once; retries stay linked to the first attempt.

A useful null is reported, not interpreted as spiritual failure. An imprecise result is inconclusive. If a valid interval excludes a prespecified meaningful effect, it constrains that operational effect under these conditions. No significance alone means proof; no nonsignificance alone means equivalence.

## 9. What outcomes discriminate?

| Outcome | Interpretation and next action |
| --- | --- |
| Better trained-person recall in either arm, hidden targets at chance | Consistent with learning, retrieval and ordinary inference. |
| M improves known-information performance, confidence or felt connection, hidden targets at chance | Psychological instruction effect; does not demonstrate transfer. |
| Correct obscure biography or Einstein-like insights | Investigate prior exposure, inference, culture and AI familiarity. These are not hidden-assignment hits. |
| Apparent effect disappears when leakage, timing or scoring errors are corrected | Compromised evidence for the original test; report the failure and all affected trials. |
| MT passes chance, but H2 fails | No prespecified mettā-specific signal. A chance-model departure, if clean, merits audit, not selection of a preferred explanation. |
| Both arms reproducibly exceed chance | Could motivate investigation of target correspondence, but contradicts a claim of mettā necessity if that claim was prospectively committed. |
| H1 and H2 pass with clean custody | Prespecified pilot signal for replication. Expectancy/attention mechanisms and residual access routes remain; no historical identity established. |
| Replicated positive interaction in a later powered study | Discriminates specified representation-dependent and representation-independent statistical models; familiarization also changes attention and strategy. |
| Clean null with narrow interval excluding the signed effect | Evidence against that effect-size prediction under the frozen conditions. Does not refute every unquantified metaphysical variant. |
| Any positive result with identical predictions under a 7D-source model and generic anomalous-access model | Does not discriminate those models. Both need different prospective likelihoods or interventions. |

A genuine model comparison requires competitors to specify different distributions before results. Source authentication needs predictions unavailable to generic access accounts, not merely accurate or moving prose. A sealed historical challenge could constrain ordinary access if exceptionally well authenticated, yet it would still not uniquely identify a communicator if a rival model predicts the same answers. This pilot therefore makes **no source-identity success criterion**.

Future sender/no-sender, real/fictional attribution or pre/post-selection manipulations could address narrower models, but each changes the tested proposition and requires independent registration and review. No unblinded extension is authorized by this candidate.

## 10. Neuroscience: what it can and cannot establish

- Distributed memory is compatible with localized contributions. Roy and colleagues mapped and manipulated engram ensembles across multiple regions in a mouse fear-memory paradigm [N1]. This is experimental evidence about that model system, not a readable atlas of a human person's knowledge.
- Human medial-temporal neurons can respond selectively across different images and names associated with individuals or concepts [N2]. Such selectivity does not mean one cell contains a whole person, their memories or a transferable informational identity.
- In a controlled human museum-memory experiment, reactivation supported both accurate memory and incorporation of misleading details, with neural measures of these processes [N3]. Vividness and confidence therefore cannot authenticate provenance; the present study records them separately from correctness.
- Tang and colleagues reconstructed aspects of meaning from fMRI with subject-specific training and cooperation [N4]. This demonstrates constrained decoding from measured living-brain signals. It is not unrestricted mind reading, recovery from historical anatomy, or evidence of an external source.
- Falk, Lepore and Noe studied Einstein's cortical anatomy through historical photographs [N5]. Anatomical observations are not a complete synaptic connectome, lifetime activity record or decoded memory inventory. Inferring that a similar-looking neural arrangement would reproduce his knowledge is unsupported by this material.

Behavioral dossier learning can operationalize available person knowledge. It cannot establish that two people have equivalent neural “nodes.” Even a connectome would not, by itself, supply the full learned synaptic states, dynamics and validated decoding model needed for that inference. This is a limitation of the proposed inference, not a claim to have proven that every future decoding method is impossible.

There is no justified conversion here from a gross brain region or named “node” to an exact number of memories or bytes. Capacity estimates depend on coding and task assumptions; do not report a total Einstein-memory capacity from photographs. No brain stimulation, scanning, neural transplant, drug intervention or attempt to copy a brain is included.

## 11. Keep 7D mathematics and physical storage separate

A seven-coordinate mathematical model can be well defined without seven physical spatial dimensions or a verified storage substrate. Dimensions in a feature vector, optical encoding degrees of freedom and spacetime coordinates are different constructs. Neither sacred geometric imagery nor a framework's name measures physical dimensionality.

A separate physics candidate would need: explicit state variables and units; a storage substrate; encoding and decoding rules; dynamics and coupling to neural activity; noise, energy and capacity constraints; a measured readout; rival predictions; and a quantitative condition that would count against it. For M distinguishable equiprobable states, log2(M) bits describes an ideal discrete encoding, not an infinite physical store. Adding coordinates does not supply those missing measurements.

No EEG theta/gamma correlation, semantic resemblance, subjective connection or target hit in this study establishes those physical requirements. Unless a physical model supplies unique prospective predictions, its comparison remains **not identifiable**, rather than supported or disproven.

## 12. Ethics, governance and reporting

Keep identities and contact details separate from response records. Fix lawful retention, deletion, access and withdrawal rules before enrollment; default to aggregate publication and controlled raw-data access. Public GitHub contains the protocol and synthetic examples only, never live targets, salts, private correspondence, consent records or participant narratives.

Use the exact PSI evidence lanes from issue #354: **Established Evidence / Interpretation / Lived Experience-Testimony / Hypothesis / Speculation**. Source-specific neuroscientific findings do not transfer their status to the information-access hypothesis.
