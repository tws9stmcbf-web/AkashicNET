# Session scripts for review

**Materials version:** 0.2-candidate, supporting protocol 0.1.  
**State:** DRAFT / REVIEW_REQUIRED / NOT FOR PARTICIPANT USE.

These are AI-assisted proposed scripts, not approved consent forms, a validated meditation intervention or evidence of information transfer. The proponent has accepted the hidden-symbol bridge only. Instruction fidelity, necessary conditions, minimum meaningful effect, ethics and independent methods review remain open. This document specifies text and timing; audio files and a tested response application do not yet exist.

## Shared participant explanation

Read identically before allocation or method disclosure:

> This study compares two ways of attending to information about historical public figures. It includes ordinary learning questions and a separate task involving randomly stored symbols. Neither method is known to reveal hidden targets. An impression, confidence or a feeling of connection does not establish contact with a person. You may report no impression, skip a response or stop. Your compensation does not depend on accuracy.

This explanation supplements an institution-approved information sheet and consent process; it does not replace either. Do not recruit or administer it until the review gates are complete.

## Day 0: comprehension and learning

1. Complete the approved consent and eligibility process. Explain the four symbol labels in this order: circle, triangle, square, plus; these are codes 0, 1, 2, 3. Shape, text and accessible labels must identify the same canonical code.
2. Present visible circle, then visible square, with all four response choices. The correct shape stays visible. If either response is incorrect or missing, use the single repair explanation below, then visible triangle and visible plus. Both repair responses must be correct to pass. Record all screening outcomes before arm randomization. These are public comprehension examples, never hidden trials.
3. After the concealed arm allocation, collect baseline familiarity and the field-of-work question for all eight names, before dossier learning. Use an independently recorded person order. No answer feedback, internet or AI assistance.
4. Present eight learning slots in a new, recorded person order. Exactly four T dossiers and four U cues are assigned independently of arm, baseline answers and targets.
5. Each slot lasts 180 seconds: T shows the full dossier for 150 seconds, then its two open retrieval questions for 15 seconds each with the dossier hidden. No corrective feedback. U shows the name and neutral cue for 15 seconds, then the unrelated filler below for 165 seconds. This clarifies that the original three-minute learning period includes retrieval. Log actual display durations and interruptions; poor learning does not remove anyone.
6. Between days, ask participants not to look up or discuss the named figures or study materials before the next session. Do not request private browsing histories. On return, record reported outside exposure; retain participants in the planned analysis. Such exposure is a learning-manipulation deviation, not proof of concealed-target leakage.

Repair explanation:

> Choose the same symbol as the one visibly shown. The written name and the shape refer to the same choice. This part checks the response interface; it does not test an unusual ability.

U filler, identical in every U slot: count the occurrences of the letter A in this fixed sequence. No shapes, person information, score or feedback are shown. Participants may stop counting and wait. Repetition and fatigue are review concerns; this task is a proposed time-filler, not a validated workload match.

A C B C A B A C B A C B C A B C B A C B A C A B
C B A C A B C A B C B A C B A B C A C B A C B A
B C A B A C B C A B C A C B A C A B C A B C B A

If a filler answer is recorded, it is descriptive only. Its ordinary answer key is in the material manifest. It is never used for screening or scoring hidden targets.

## Baseline and return questionnaires

Use the same wording and response options in both arms. Every optional questionnaire permits “prefer not to answer.”

- Familiarity, per name: “Before this study, how familiar were you with this person?” 0 never heard of them; 1 recognize the name; 2 know a little; 3 know several facts; 4 know their work well.
- Field-of-work inventory: use the exact person-specific free-response item in stimulus-set.json. Do not display its accepted-answer examples.
- General exposure: “In the past month, have you used an AI chatbot to discuss historical people?” yes / no / unsure / prefer not to answer. Ask the same four-choice item for reading, watching or listening to biographies. Do not collect account names or chat transcripts.
- Return exposure, per name: “Since the first session, did you encounter information about this person outside the study?” yes / no / unsure / prefer not to answer. Optional source class: reading / video or audio / conversation / AI / other; no URLs or private details required.
- Before and after the method audio: “Out of 100 concealed choices like these, how many do you expect you would match?” integer 0–100 / unsure / prefer not to answer. Explain beforehand that there are four equally likely symbols. Do not call a higher expectation a better answer.

At the 24 ± 4 hour return, present the eight delayed recognition items before the method audio, with a 20-second limit per item and no feedback. These ask about award years; they do not repeat the final factual-recall questions. Both T and U see the same limited set of candidate years, so U is not claimed to have zero information. Record that common exposure. Randomize option positions independently of hidden targets. Include “do not know” outside the four substantive options. A missing/incorrect response scores zero for the descriptive knowledge check.

## Five-minute method audio

Use the same consenting speaker, recording setup, volume and neutral delivery for both tracks, with no background music. Each of the following six slots lasts exactly 50 seconds. Read its text once at the beginning, then leave silence for the remainder. No added suggestions, reassurance or target cues. Total: 300 seconds. Review and hash the actual recordings before registration; text timing alone does not prove audio equivalence.

The participant experiences the content and therefore is not blind to method. Use neutral track labels X/Y for staff and analysts; retain the private mapping until the approved unmasking point. A blind facilitator does not make this a fully double-blind intervention.

## Shared hidden-task explanation

> A computer has randomly stored one of four symbols in the sealed record for each person and trial. Try to identify that symbol. This is a new experimental association; it is not a claim about the person's actual thoughts or possessions. Every trial has a fresh, equally likely choice. Previous guesses cannot tell you which symbol was selected next. You will receive no correctness feedback.

This statement is used only after the implemented custody system has passed review. The present repository contains no actual hidden assignments.

## Trial and block prompts

- Cue, 15 seconds: name plus “Public historical figure.” Identical display for T and U. The public figure IDs are F01–F08; secret assignments never enter the response app.
- Free response, up to 60 seconds: “What, if anything, came to mind? You may write 'no impression'. Avoid private or identifying details.” A blank is permitted. Lock the original text before showing the symbol choices.
- Choice, 15 seconds: “Select one symbol, or select abstain.” Display all four labels/shapes in a new independent spatial permutation. No default selection; the first explicit confirmation locks the code. A timeout retains its own status.
- Ratings, up to 45 seconds total: confidence 0–100; source attribution memory / inference / spontaneous impression / other / unsure; felt connection 0–10. All optional; missing ratings cannot alter a locked choice or remove a trial.
- After trials 4, 8 and 12, offer a scheduled 120-second break, followed by the assigned 60-second reminder below. Extra accessibility or distress breaks are logged. Do not insert a method reminder after trial 16, when hidden testing has finished.
- Reminder M: “Return to the person's name with goodwill and compassion. Notice what arises without treating it as a message or fact. Record it without embellishment, then choose a symbol or abstain.”
- Reminder R: “Return to the person's name and review available facts and associations. Distinguish what you know from what you are guessing. Record it without embellishment, then choose a symbol or abstain.”

Each reminder is read once at the beginning of its 60-second slot, then silence. No improvised coaching or praise for apparent accuracy. Break timing must not depend on target values.

## Ordinary tasks after all hidden trials

First present all eight dossier-recall items, then all eight fictional-inference items, in independently recorded person orders. Do not interleave inference and recall: a fictional exercise must not prime a later factual-recall answer. Allow up to 30 seconds for each recall item and 45 seconds for each inference item. Randomize substantive option positions; provide “do not know.” Preserve the selected option ID, display order and missing status. No correctness feedback until the whole study is locked and its debrief policy permits it.

Shared inference introduction:

> The following situations are invented reasoning exercises. They are not historical documents or statements about what these people actually did. Use only the premises written in each question.

Score each task separately: exact keyed option 1, otherwise 0, with completed-response summaries and missing counts. The baseline free text remains descriptively coded under a frozen rubric. These ordinary tasks do not count as concealed-information successes.

## Neutral debrief draft

> This study compared attention instructions, learning and choices about concealed random symbols. A match, mismatch or feeling of connection does not authenticate communication with a historical person. We will evaluate all planned responses together, including missing responses and errors. Results will be reported after the planned checks and independent review. Your experience and your score are not measures of spiritual worth or a diagnosis.

Investigator contact, support arrangements, withdrawal deadline and data-release explanation must be supplied by the responsible institution. Do not invent contact details or promise deletion rights inconsistent with the approved policy.
