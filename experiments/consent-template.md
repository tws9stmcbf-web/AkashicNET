# Informed Consent Template (Citizen Science)

Use this plain-language template when collecting participant data for citizen-science projects. Adapt to local legal requirements and institutional review when relevant.

Title: [Project title]

Purpose
- Briefly describe the purpose of the project and what participation involves.

What we will collect
- Describe the data types (e.g., surveys, time-series self-report, sensor-derived measures, images) and any metadata (timestamp, pseudonymised id, context tags).

How data will be used
- Explain that data will be used for research, hypothesis generation, and may be shared in anonymised form for reproducibility. Explain any planned publications or public summaries.

Voluntary participation
- Participation is voluntary. Participants can withdraw at any time. Describe how to request deletion of their data.

Privacy and anonymisation
- Describe how personal data will be minimised, pseudonymised or anonymised. State retention period and deletion workflow (e.g., data retained for X years unless participant requests deletion).

Risks and benefits
- Describe foreseeable risks and any potential benefits. For sensitive interventions (health, substances, strong psychological practices), recommend professional supervision and note that the Toolkit is not medical advice.

Consent statement
- By signing or clicking "I consent" the participant confirms they have read this information, had the opportunity to ask questions, and agree to participate under the terms described.

Contact & withdrawal
- Provide contact details for questions and instructions for withdrawing consent and requesting data deletion.

Date: [ISO 8601]

Signature: [digital/pseudonymous identifier]
