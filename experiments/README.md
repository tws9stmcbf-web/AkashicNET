# 🧪 Experiments

A space for structured experiments, citizen-science projects and N-of-1 observations related to consciousness, wellbeing and human potential.

## 🔬 Experimental Cycle

**Question → Hypothesis → Method → Observation → Analysis → Replication → Learning**

## 📋 Experiment Records

Where practical, document:

- Research question
- Hypothesis
- Background evidence
- Participants or sample
- Method
- Baseline
- Measures
- Observations
- Potential confounders
- Results
- Limitations
- Replication status
- Conclusions

## 🧠 N-of-1 Observations

Individual observations can generate useful hypotheses but should not automatically be treated as generalisable evidence.

Clearly distinguish:

**Personal observation ≠ population-level evidence**

## ⚖️ Safety & Ethics

Experiments should minimise unnecessary risk.

Research involving health conditions, medication, psychoactive substances or significant psychological interventions requires appropriate safeguards and, where relevant, qualified professional oversight.

The Toolkit does not replace medical, psychological or scientific advice.

## 🔁 Reproducibility

Whenever possible, experiments should provide enough information for another person to understand and reproduce the procedure.

Negative or inconclusive results are valuable and should not be excluded simply because they do not support the original hypothesis.

## 🌱 Purpose

The purpose of experimentation is not to prove a predetermined worldview.

It is to **learn what survives careful observation**.
