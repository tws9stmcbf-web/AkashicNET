## Interpretation and integration

Read each entry's `reported_outcomes.attributed_to` alongside its limitations. AIR separates laboratory findings from operational utility; Utts and Hyman's differing conclusions must retain their names. The operational selection deliberately includes contrasting assessments from different cases and periods. No programme-wide success rate is calculated.

The index maps to the existing **RV discovery category**. Target-person and sender protocols require separate TEL classification before any trial-level evidence assignment. Neither archival custody, intelligence interest, funding nor declassification establishes scientific validity.

Book descriptions and retrospective accounts do not substitute for original records. ## Access, rights and next review
