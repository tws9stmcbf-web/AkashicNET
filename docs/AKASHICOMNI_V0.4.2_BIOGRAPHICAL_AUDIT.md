# AkashicOMNI v0.4.2 — biographical source audit

Date: 19 September 2026. Status: editorial audit and proposed corrections; no new release published by this audit.

## Finding

The eight cases can support a reflective comparison of lives and work, but the current citations do not support every field equally. Four context accounts are supported at their stated level (Ramanujan, Tesla as testimony, Pascal and Hawking). Babbage needs clearer attribution; Kepler has a citation gap; Faraday needs a working link and separate health evidence; Darwin needs a specific source and narrower duration wording unless further records are inspected.

This is an AI-assisted source audit, not independent human review, clinical validation or evidence that embodiment causes wisdom. Institutional biography remains secondary evidence. A first-person account documents what its author reported; it does not independently establish every reported event or explanation. Retrieval errors below are access limitations, not proof that a page is permanently broken.

## Changes justified by the audit

1. Attribute Babbage's health story to his recollection. Replace the banner's “ESTABLISHED BIOGRAPHICAL CONTEXT” with “BIOGRAPHICAL ACCOUNT” and its claim with “Babbage recalled schooling in Alphington after childhood illness.”
2. Repair Faraday's source URL and separate contribution evidence from health evidence. Mark the research paper as abstract-only until its full text and cited records are inspected.
3. Give Darwin a direct letter citation and narrow the duration claim to the inspected evidence. Additional dated records could support a longer chronology later.
4. Add a source for Kepler's health claim or explicitly mark it unresolved. Do not silently pass it as verified.
5. Change the shared heading “Intelligence expressed” to “Interpretive description of work”. Terms such as “internal modelling”, “symbolic compression” and “spatial abstraction” are editorial interpretations here, not psychometric findings.
6. Separate each case's source for life context from its source for scientific contribution. A biography link should not imply support for every adjacent statement.
7. State that selecting celebrated achievers is not representative sampling. The case series has no comparison group and measures neither wisdom nor a causal relationship between illness and achievement.

These are corrections and clarifications of the existing pathway and would justify a v0.4.3 patch when applied and checked. This audit itself does not change the current v0.4.2 release or promote the v0.5.0 draft.

## HIERATIC015C1 — illustrative application

This is a new hypothetical example, not an amendment to an unseen HIERATIC015C1 manuscript or an empirical study.

A discussion becomes tense. A participant notices a tightening jaw and an urge to interrupt.

- **Embodied observation:** “I notice tension and an urge to speak.” This is a report of present experience.
- **DISCERN:** “I think the other person is dismissing me.” That is an interpretation; the bodily sensation does not establish the other person's intention.
- **DEEPEN:** Consider fatigue, misunderstanding, urgency and competing concerns. Ask what was meant and listen to the answer.
- **Compassionate action:** Pause, state the concern without accusation, and agree a next step that respects both people.
- **Review:** Did understanding improve? Did the response reduce harm? What feedback would change the interpretation?

Embodied awareness supplies a cue; evidence, dialogue and ethical reflection shape the response. Calling that response wise is an evaluative judgement that should remain open to consequences and correction. Exceptional scientific achievement is not itself a measure of wisdom.

## Remaining limits

No originating Babbage edition, original Tesla magazine scans, Faraday clinical notes, full Larner paper or independent human assessment was completed. Kepler's health claim remains unsupported by its current citation. Broad contribution summaries were checked only to the extent described in the table; this is not a full audit of the scientists' original research. No clinical or causal conclusions follow from this source check.
