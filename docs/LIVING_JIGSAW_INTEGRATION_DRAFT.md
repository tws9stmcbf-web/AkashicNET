## Questions for the human editor

1. **Does the candidate stand alone as a question?** It must be understandable without relying on a framework coordinate or label.
2. **Does it change action or inquiry?** Request a one-sentence question, a concrete example and a reason it differs from its nearest neighbour. Where these cannot be supplied, revise or set aside the entry.
3. **What is the relationship type?** Use *same question*, *overlap*, *applies to*, *contrasts with*, or *needs review*. “Combine” requires a named target and a written rationale; it should not silently delete provenance.
4. **Does a change touch a published identifier?** Preserve the original Jigsaw and HUMAN 2.0 references. The Living Jigsaw can map to them without rewriting their published history.
5. **Which links must remain visible?** Every keep, combination or set-aside decision must mention relevant Jigsaw neighbours and matrix contexts. A combination inherits the source IDs and links of every piece it contains.

## Consolidation screen

Before fixing the final count, review the retained questions with four possible dispositions: **keep separate, combine, link, or set aside**. “Approved” means retained for this screen; it does not guarantee that the question remains a separate final piece.

Every consolidation proposal must show:

- the original Jigsaw source IDs and questions;
- any proposed combined wording;
- links to neighbouring Jigsaw questions;
- links to Living Spectrum lenses and 7 × 13 matrix contexts;
- the reason to combine or remain separate.

A link is not a merger. Combined pieces must preserve all source identifiers, publication history and inherited links.

## Suggested review order

1. **Definition gate: complete.** Only standalone, human-reviewed questions qualify as pieces.
2. **47-piece pass:** review the seven neighbourhoods and five connectors for distinctness, usefulness and missing questions. Mark publication links separately from editorial decisions.
3. **Consolidation screen:** test retained questions for keep, combine, link or set-aside decisions while explicitly listing all relevant links.
4. **91-cell pass:** by lens, check whether each generated intersection yields a meaningful, non-redundant standalone question. “Not reviewed” remains the default; no automatic promotion into a piece.
5. **Cross-links:** document exact counterparts, useful applications and genuine merges with stable source IDs.
6. **Human sign-off:** only then publish a new count and visual map, with a changelog of additions, combinations and set-aside entries.
