## 🚀 Phase 7 — Possible Futures Lab

Create an evidence-labelled pathway from technologies that exist today to increasingly speculative future scenarios. Every scenario should distinguish **Established Evidence**, **Emerging Research**, **Engineering Extrapolation**, **Hypothesis**, and **Speculation**.

### Regenerative and fungal architecture

- [ ] Catalogue peer-reviewed mycelium-composite research and built prototypes.
- [ ] Explore carbon-storing / lower-carbon building envelopes, insulation, acoustic panels and non-structural components made from fungal mycelium and agricultural residues.
- [ ] Track durability, moisture sensitivity, fire performance, lifecycle impacts, energy used for sterilisation/drying, scalability and building-code barriers.
- [ ] Explore hybrid architecture in which conventional structural frames are combined with bio-grown insulation, cladding and infill.
- [ ] Maintain a **Fungal Unknowns** research frontier: most fungal diversity remains scientifically undescribed, so possible future materials should be framed as discovery opportunities rather than assumed capabilities.
- [ ] Explore longer-horizon ideas such as self-growing, repairable or adaptive biomaterials only as explicitly labelled research hypotheses/speculation until demonstrated.

### Planetary futures

- [ ] Regenerative cities and circular material systems.
- [ ] Distributed renewable energy, storage and resilient local infrastructure.
- [ ] Ecological restoration, biodiversity sensing and nature-positive design.
- [ ] AI-assisted scientific discovery with transparent provenance and human oversight.
- [ ] Closed-loop food, water and waste systems.

### Interplanetary and Kardashev futures

- [ ] Space habitats using locally available resources and bio-derived materials where technically plausible.
- [ ] Large-scale solar-energy systems and Dyson-swarm concepts as engineering extrapolations rather than predictions.
- [ ] Long-duration robotic and eventually human interstellar exploration scenarios.
- [ ] Treat Kardashev Type II/III and beyond-Kardashev civilizations as thought experiments constrained by known physics, energy budgets and explicit uncertainty.
- [ ] Keep wormholes, multiverse travel, time manipulation and similar concepts in a clearly labelled speculative layer unless evidence changes.

## 💡 Future AkashicNET Project Plan — Funding Required

AkashicNET's public knowledge should remain freely accessible. Funding expands the project's capacity; it does not purchase scientific certainty, privileged conclusions or evidence promotion.

### Stage A — Evidence infrastructure

- [ ] Expand provenance-aware knowledge graph and source monitoring.
- [ ] Build reusable Future Scenario records linking claims to evidence and counter-evidence.
- [ ] Add structured technology-readiness and uncertainty labels.
- [ ] Improve accessibility, responsive design and public data exports.

### Stage B — Fungal Futures Atlas

- [ ] Partner with mycologists, biomaterials researchers, architects and lifecycle specialists.
- [ ] Catalogue species/material/substrate combinations from published research without implying untested species have useful construction properties.
- [ ] Map built mycelium architecture case studies and reproducibility gaps.
- [ ] Develop interactive visualisations connecting fungal biodiversity, materials science, carbon, architecture and circular design.

### Stage C — Possible Futures Simulator

- [ ] Interactive scenarios from 2030 → 2050 → 2100 → deep future.
- [ ] Let visitors vary energy, materials, biodiversity, AI, governance and space-development assumptions.
- [ ] Show consequences, dependencies, uncertainties and failure modes rather than presenting a single utopia as inevitable.
- [ ] Link every evidence-dependent claim back to its provenance record.

### Stage D — Open collaborations

- [ ] Research partnerships and expert review.
- [ ] Student and citizen-science participation where ethically appropriate.
- [ ] Open datasets, reproducible notebooks and public scenario challenges.
- [ ] Grants and voluntary community support to fund hosting, research tooling, data stewardship, accessibility and commissioned expert work.

## ♾️ Long-Term Vision

Build an open, transparent and evolving living library for exploring consciousness, science, nature, technology and possible futures without requiring a predetermined worldview.

**Explore → Question → Test → Learn → Connect → Imagine → Improve**

**Fund the question — not the answer.**
