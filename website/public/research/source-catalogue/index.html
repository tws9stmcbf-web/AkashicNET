Scope and provenance

Source titles, authors, displayed dates, links and article licence notices are separated from AI-generated discovery notes and proposed BQ mappings. Web retrieval can return cached text. Displayed page dates are not dates of the studies discussed. Licence notices apply to the named articles and do not automatically cover datasets or third-party material. No article bodies are included.
