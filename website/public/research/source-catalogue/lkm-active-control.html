Loving-kindness: active-control appraisal

CM-OA-012 · Q004 · Checked 2 October 2026 · Preliminary AI-assisted review

Interpretation: this comparison supplies a relevant null result. It tests different outcomes and a different population from the depression trial, so it is not a direct replication.

DesignFour-week loving-kindness versus progressive muscle relaxation; 75 enrolled, 69 final sample. Healthy US adults.
Pain resultNo demonstrated advantage for loving-kindness; neither arm improved the tested pain outcomes over time.
Social supportReported comparison favoured relaxation (p=.028). Multiplicity needs checking against alpha=.0125 in the power discussion.
LimitationsPandemic setting; remote wall-sit task; incomplete follow-up; no passive control. Null results do not establish equivalence.
RegistrationNCT04632875; OSF preregistration dated after recruitment began. Full registry comparison pending.
