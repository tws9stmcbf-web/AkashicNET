# AKASHICNET.ORG brand standard

## Canonical name

Use **AKASHICNET.ORG** for the public-facing website identity.

Use **AkashicNET** when referring to the wider project, repository, knowledge network or software architecture.

## Visual direction

The identity moves from organic to technological:

1. living seed, heart and tree;
2. toroidal field and reciprocal flow;
3. network nodes and signal arcs;
4. precise spacing and human-governed technology.

## Primary emblem

**Sacred Toroidal Love** — an organic “love Wi-Fi mast”: a heart-seed/tree held within a toroidal transmission field, with gold structure, emerald/mint flow and restrained violet connection points.

The mark communicates compassionate intelligence and interconnection. It does not imply a scientific proof of metaphysical claims.

## Typography

Pair an organic literary serif with disciplined uppercase spacing:

- display and philosophical language: organic serif;
- navigation, metadata and system labels: precise uppercase with generous tracking;
- canonical wordmark: **AKASHICNET.ORG**.

## Relationship to NeuronsToNirvana

The Sacred Toroidal Love emblem is the AkashicNET identity. The NeuronsToNirvana brain-to-lotus mark remains the distinct community identity on community sections.
